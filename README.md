# One Letter for Your Day — K, Knackered

One letter, K, standing for "knackered": flat-out exhausted after a day spent grinding make.fun bounties from first light to last. A sharp, specific picture of a real day — vivid, funny, and true.

Entry for the make.fun bounty [One Letter for Your Day](https://make.fun/bounty/u-puan-one-letter-for-your-day-e54e9c36).

## How it works

`main.ts` writes exactly one character, `K`, to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, no trailing newline, no extra text). Nothing else is written.
